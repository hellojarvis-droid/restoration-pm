## Five differentiation pillars (your weapons)

1. **Voice-first input** with Ontario domain vocabulary ("TRESA, FINTRAC, OREA, Form 300, holdback, status certificate, Kitec, aluminum wiring, special assessment, reserve fund")
2. **SHA-256 hash-chain audit log** with public daily anchor (tamper-evident for RECO 7-year retention)
3. **Self-consistency + citation-grounded accuracy** (every claim cites source page + verbatim quote ≤200 chars; independent Haiku verification pass)
4. **Paralegal/lawyer professional-channel exclusivity** (signed LOIs, 12-month exclusivity with founding firms; this is what well-funded competitors can't replicate)
5. **Adjudicator-specific outcome intelligence + comp-condo data corpus** (data that compounds with every customer use)

## Strategic positioning summary

**FormFlow does NOT compete with:**
- Ownright (closing-side legal services with employed lawyers)
- Mave AI (agent marketing + content automation; consumer-side branding)
- WEBForms (OREA's institutional form delivery system)

**FormFlow DOES compete with:**
- LTB-specific paralegal workflow tools (RentZen, LTB Ready, Mi Property Portal, LandlordEzy)
- Status Cert review AI products (Ownright, Eli, CondoDoc, StrataReports) — but only on the B2B/lawyer side
- TRESA + FINTRAC compliance tools for brokers of record (Iluminai, Fintracker, SkySlope SmartAudit)

**FormFlow's unique position:** The only product that ties all three (LTB + Status Cert + TRESA) together through the same channel partners (paralegals → lawyers → brokers of record), with cross-customer data aggregation as the compounding moat.
