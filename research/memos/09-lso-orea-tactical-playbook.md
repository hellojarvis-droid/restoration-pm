## Critical Strategic Decision: Bifurcate the Product in the Application

- **TRESA Co-Pilot (B2B for licensed agents/brokers):** Outside A2I scope. Not consumer-facing, not legal services to public; B2B SaaS to RECO-licensed registrants whose own regulation covers them.
- **Status Cert Analyzer + LTB Hearing Prep (consumer-facing):** A2I application target. Position like Philer AI: AI extracts and structures; licensed lawyer reviews and certifies.

## LSO A2I Key Facts

- **Authority:** By-Law 16 — "deems approved participants in the A2I program not to be practising law or providing legal services with respect to the operation of the approved tool."
- **Pilot end date:** November 2026 (originally 5-year pilot Nov 2021 → Nov 2026). Future uncertain but participation agreements are 2-year auto-converting to 1-year rolling — approval today still buys ~24 months safe-harbour.
- **Process:** Stage 1 (concept review) → Stage 2 (product review with extra cyber/privacy scrutiny). Email submission to `accesstoinnovation@LSO.ca`. Rolling intake, no fee disclosed.
- **Realistic timeline:** 4-6 months from Stage 1 to participation agreement signature. Hybrid AI-plus-licensee applications get longer Stage 2 reviews.
- **Insurance required:** $1M general liability, $1M E&O, $500K cyber. LSO named as additional insured.
- **Cybersecurity:** Within 2 years must demonstrate ISO 27001, NIST 800-53, SOC 2 or equivalent.
- **Reporting cadence:** Quarterly data report. 5-business-day notice for fraud/wilful-misconduct/gross-negligence complaints. 72-hour breach notification.

## Critical Insight from the June 2025 Convocation Report

> "Often the evaluation process results in changes to applicants' insurance coverage, terms and conditions, internal complaints processes, privacy policies and, in certain instances, applicants' entire business models in a manner that adds protections consistent with licensees' professional requirements."

**A2I doesn't reject — it negotiates.** Applications stall when applicants resist changes to T&Cs (especially liability caps and class-action waivers) or cannot meet the insurance floor.

## OREA Forms License Key Facts

- **Authority:** OREA owns copyright on every numbered standard form. License is "discretionary" — no published fee schedule, no SLA.
- **Single contact:** `standardforms@orea.com`. No portal, no application form.
- **Only legal-profession licensee:** LDD (LawyerDoneDeal) has a monopoly on the legal-side channel.
- **Member-facing licensees (verified from OREA's published list):** Dotloop, NexOne, CREA WEBForms (Lone Wolf), DocEM, SkySlope, myAbode.
- **Mave AI has NO OREA license** — operates at 8,500+ agents (Real Estate Magazine 2025) by avoiding form bytes entirely. This is the proof point that "ship without license" works at scale.
- **Probability of OREA granting license to solo non-developer founder:** <40%.

## The Fallback Architecture (Build As If Denied)

Three layers, in order of regulatory safety:

1. **Clauses-only output.** Generate clause text (financing condition, status-cert review, escape clauses, irrevocability period explanations) and instruct user to paste into their own WEBForms session. RunSensible and Mave do this at scale; no enforcement action documented.
2. **Browser handoff (Stagehand-style automation of user's own session).** Drives registrant's own WEBForms login to populate fields. Copyright-neutral; ToS-grey but defensible.
3. **Independent RECO-owned forms.** RECO Information Guide and SRP Acknowledgement are RECO documents, not OREA-owned. Any tool can use them freely.
