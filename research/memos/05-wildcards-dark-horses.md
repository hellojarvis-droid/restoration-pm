## The 6-Point Filter

1. AI-native problem infeasible before frontier LLMs
2. Sharp, mandatory pain in a paying buyer
3. Self-serve B2B onboarding (no enterprise sales motion)
4. ACV $1k+/mo achievable
5. TAM math: 50k+ buyers globally → $50M+ ARR at <10% penetration
6. Solo-operable: AI handles support; distribution is SEO/integration/community

## Deep Dive #1: Land Surveyor Plat Review (Conf 72)

- ~50,000 US licensed PLSs, ~200,000 globally; median firm <5 people
- Vision-LLMs + geometry parsing finally unlock the work: read metes-and-bounds prose, reconstruct polygons, cross-check ALTA Table A, flag closure errors
- Mandatory license-stamp liability creates non-discretionary pain
- ACV $300–1,000/mo per surveyor
- TAM: 50k × 10% × $6k/yr = $30M floor; with international + adjacent buyers (title cos, RE attorneys) comfortably $100M+

**Verified competition (THIN):**
- [Survey XYZ](https://surveyxyz.com) — exact wedge, very early stage, no pricing
- [Bunting Labs](https://buntinglabs.com) — QGIS plugin, developer-tilted
- [AirWorks](https://blog.airworks.io) — CAD/aerial, not legal-text
- [DeedPlotter AI](https://deedplotter.ai) — county-clerk side, not surveyor

[HeroX ALTA Land Survey Automation Challenge](https://www.herox.com/AiLTA) confirms gap is industry-recognized and unfilled.

**Risk:** older buyer pool, regional fragmentation, 2–3 years of community brand-building before viral adoption. Defensible $100M ARR business in ~5–7 years; not a 12-month rocket.

## Deep Dive #2: ITAR/EAR Small Manufacturer Compliance (Conf 70)

- ~12,000 ITAR-registered DDTC entities + ~50,000 small EAR manufacturers (semiconductors, optics, electronics, dual-use chemicals)
- IEEPA civil penalties up to $364k/violation; ITAR criminal exposure 20 years + $1M/violation
- Outsourced compliance: $30–150k/year per shop
- ACV $1,500–5,000/mo SaaS prices 75% below consultant alternative
- January 2025 [AI Diffusion Framework](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/01/15/2025-00636/) expanded the touched universe

**Verified competition:**
- [Export Orbital](https://www.exportorbital.ai) — one early AI-native, small-manufacturer-focused
- [Pathlock](https://pathlock.com/regulatory/itar-ear-compliance/) — enterprise tier
- Descartes / 3rdwave / Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE — Fortune 500 only

Frontier LLMs can now: (a) classify part drawings to ECCN/USML, (b) screen end-users in seconds, (c) draft Technology Control Plans, (d) maintain audit-grade reasoning chains.

**Risk:** SaaS vendor inherits some classification liability — needs careful ToS and human-review tier.

## Implication for the Top-Level Thesis

The two strongest wildcards (land surveyor, ITAR) both score below the AI-infra winner (Browser/computer-use reliability middleware, conf 80). But they are the strongest **vertical** plays for a founder whose comparative advantage is domain expertise or vertical credibility rather than dev-tools building. They are the backup picks if the founder cannot ship production developer SDKs.
