# Memo 7: LTB Hearing Prep AI (Dark Horse Winner)

**Date:** May 16, 2026
**Verdict:** Confidence 82/100. Strongest pain density of any researched niche.
**Role in the stack:** Stage 1 — 30-day cashflow validation wedge before scaling to OREA Forms.

## The acute pain

- Ontario LTB (Landlord & Tenant Board) backlog: **8–14 months** for L1 (non-payment) hearings
- Average lost rent per non-paying tenant: $30k+
- Paralegal fees per hearing: $1,500–$4,000
- ~10,000 LTB hearings per year (Ontario only)
- Small landlords (3-30 units) overwhelmingly self-represent because paralegals erase a year of cash flow

## The product

Upload:
- The lease
- N4 (notice to terminate) + L1 (application)
- Tenant ledger (rent payment history)
- Prior text/email communications

AI generates:
- Hearing-ready evidence binder with tabbed exhibits
- Witness-style opening statement
- Cross-examination question bank against likely tenant defenses (s.82 cross-claims, maintenance counterclaims)
- Chronology timeline

## Pricing

- $499 per hearing (event-driven)
- $99/mo SaaS for active landlords (3+ units)

## Distribution — free

- **SOLO Facebook group** (Small Ownership Landlords of Ontario): 30,000+ members
- **r/OntarioLandlord**: active subreddit, frequent LTB threads
- **Ontario Landlords Association**: paid sponsorship channel
- **Paralegal partnerships**: referral kickback for paralegals who can't take a case

## Zero verified incumbents

- AppFolio, Buildium, RentRedi, Yardi Breeze — PM workflow, no LTB specialization
- Doormat — lawyer-side, full conveyancing, not LTB prep
- SOLO Network, OLA — advocacy non-profits, not software
- No AI-native LTB prep tool exists as of May 2026

## Vibes-codeable difficulty: 9/10

- Every LTB form is public PDF (downloadable from Tribunals Ontario)
- Every relevant statute (RTA, Rules of Practice) is public
- Prior CanLII LTB decisions train a strong retrieval layer
- Claude can absolutely draft an opening statement and evidence index
- Hardest engineering: OCR of tenant ledgers from QuickBooks/Buildium exports — solvable

## Ontario-only ARR ceiling

- 10,000 hearings/year × $499 = **$5M ARR from hearings alone**
- 800 active landlords × $99/mo = **+$0.95M ARR**
- **Realistic solo ceiling: $6–10M ARR Ontario-only by Year 3**

## Continental expansion difficulty: HIGH

- BC RTB, NY Housing Court, NJ Special Civil, MA Housing Court, IL Cook County eviction — each requires separate forms/statutes/precedent corpora
- Estimated $30–50M ARR continental ceiling by Year 5 (vs. $150–200M for OREA Forms)

## Why this is Stage 1, not the main play

LTB Hearing Prep is the **cashflow validation wedge** — it proves the founder can:
1. Find Ontario landlord customers
2. Charge real money for AI-generated documents
3. Maintain >85% accuracy on legal/regulatory output
4. Build a brand in Ontario real estate communities

Once $20-50K MRR is running on LTB alone, the same audience (Ontario landlords ARE Ontario real estate transactors) becomes the distribution channel for Stage 2 (Status Certificate Analyzer) and Stage 3 (OREA Forms / TRESA Co-Pilot).

## Top 3 failure modes

1. **Paralegal lobby pushes regulatory restriction** (~10%). Unlikely — small landlords legally self-represent under RTA. Mitigation: position as "research and preparation aid," not legal advice.
2. **Tenant advocacy organizations build counter-tool** (~80%). Probability is high but it doesn't kill the thesis — different buyer.
3. **LTB clears backlog** (~5%). Bill 97 (2023) didn't fix it; structural adjudicator shortage continues.
