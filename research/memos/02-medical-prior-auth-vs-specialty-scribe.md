# Memo 2: AI Prior Auth vs. Specialty Scribe — Which Sub-Niche Wins?

**Date:** May 16, 2026
**Verdict:** Specialty scribe wins (veterinary is the wedge). Confidence 22/100 for solo-$100M-ARR.
**Why:** Best structural medical niche (no HIPAA for vet, PLG buy, big tech out of scope) — but Scribenote went FREE in June 2025 with a16z backing, and there's a structural wall at $5–15M ARR for solo operators.

## Opportunity A — Provider-Side Prior Authorization

### Pain
- AMA 2025 PA Survey: physicians complete ~40 PAs/week, 13 hrs/week burden, 94% link to burnout, 24% cite serious adverse events
- Oncology denial rates ~30%; $15–30k per denied immunotherapy cycle
- Top-down TAM: ~$27B/yr labor burden

### Competition — already crowded and well-funded
- **Cohere Health**: $200M total funding, 12M PAs/yr, payer-side
- **Anterior**: $64M total, KLAS-validated 99.24% accuracy, payer-side
- **Rhyme**: 91 hospital systems, integrated with Microsoft Dragon Copilot Oct 2025 — closes provider-side niche
- **Humata Health**: $25M, 225+ hospitals, enterprise sale
- **Develop Health**: $14.3M Series A Aug 2025
- **Olive AI**: dead — $4B peak valuation, manual ops behind the curtain

### Why A is structurally wrong for solo
- Buyer mismatch: payers have the budget; provider ROI is diffuse
- CMS-0057-F mandates FHIR PA APIs by Jan 2027 — favors payer-aligned incumbents
- Liability: delayed cancer therapy = litigation-class harm
- Big tech entered: Rhyme + Dragon Copilot, Epic "Art" Q1 2026

**Verdict on A: Skip.**

## Opportunity B — Specialty Scribe (Veterinary wedge)

### Market
- US vet market $14.85B (2025) → $34.83B by 2035 (Grand View)
- ~32,000 companion-animal practices, ~2.6 FTE vets each
- 8–10 hours/week documentation burden; "pajama charting" is universal
- VetGeni: search for "veterinary AI" grew 1,680% YoY 2024→2025

### Why veterinary specifically wins among medical sub-niches
- **HIPAA does not apply** — no human PHI, no BAA scale problem
- Buyer = practice-owner vet = PLG dynamics
- Big tech explicitly out-of-scope (Dragon Copilot/Epic Art target physicians)
- PIMS integration creates moat (ezyVet API, AVImark, Cornerstone all need work)

### Competition
- **Scribenote**: $8.2M seed from a16z (Sept 2024), tripled users, launched FREE TIER June 2025
- **Talkatoo**: 14,000+ users, Patterson partnership
- **HappyDoc** $149/mo flat, **CoVet** $45–99/mo, **Otto.vet** $24/mo, **ScribbleVet** acquired by Instinct Jan 2026
- **Eleos Health** (behavioral): $120M total, ~$30–50M ARR — existence proof that specialty scribes can hit $50M+
- **Bola AI** (dental): 10K+ dentists, 99% accuracy claim
- **Overjet acquired DentalBee** Dec 2025 — EMR vendors are bundling

### Realistic ACV & TAM
- $1,200–$1,800/yr per vet → ~$60M ARR at full vet-only penetration
- $300M+ ARR requires entering 2–3 adjacent specialties (dental, optometry, behavioral)

## Kill criteria for B

1. **Race to free**: Scribenote went FREE June 2025; a16z subsidy lets it crush solo pricing
2. **PIMS-vendor preemption**: ScribbleVet→Instinct, DentalBee→Overjet — vertical EMRs absorbing scribe layer
3. **Solo capacity wall at $5–10M ARR**: corporate group sales require named CS, SOC2 audits, contract negotiation

## Realistic Outcome

- Solo to $10M ARR: plausible (~30%)
- Solo to $100M ARR: structurally impossible without breaking solo
- Acquisition exit at $20–80M ARR: ~40% likely within 4 years

**Verdict: pick if you love vet/medical workflows. Don't pick if your goal is $1B solo.**
