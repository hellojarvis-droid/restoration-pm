# Memo 3: AI Customs/HTS vs. Building Permits

**Date:** May 16, 2026
**Verdict:** Permits wins, but capped. Customs killed by regulation. Permits confidence: 62/100.

## Opportunity A — Customs / HTS Classification + Duty Drawback

### **KILLED — regulatory wall**

**CBP Ruling H350722 (Jan 16, 2026)** explicitly states an unlicensed entity using an AI tool "cannot derive HTSUS subheadings beyond the six-digit level," and "cannot decide what data should appear on an entry."

US duties live at the **10-digit** level. A solo founder must either:
- Pass CBLE (12% pass rate Oct 2025; requires 3 years experience), OR
- Hire licensed broker ($80–150k loaded cost) — kills solo economics

### Market evidence (so you know what you'd be giving up)
- ~400,000 US SMB importers (+60% since 2019)
- $11–15B unclaimed duty drawback annually (mostly concentrated in mid-market/enterprise manufacturers)
- Broker fees $35–225/entry average
- Tariff regime brutal: de minimis killed for China May 2025, all countries Aug 2025
- DOJ Trade Fraud Task Force launched Aug 2025; $50M tungsten settlement, $12.4M Allied Stone

### Why A is dead despite real pain
- Flexport launched Customs Technology Suite Fall 2025 (25+ AI products), reviewing 100% of entries; customs gross profit doubled YoY
- **Zonos** won CBP CSOP contract Nov 2025 — CBP itself uses Zonos
- **Gaia Dynamics** (Andrew Ng's AI Fund, $2M seed): 92% HTS accuracy claim, 100% on broker exam
- Amazon bundles licensed-broker HTS classification FREE for FBA sellers
- ATLAS benchmark (arXiv 2509.18400): best fine-tuned LLM gets 40% fully correct 10-digit (not deployable without human-in-loop)

**Verdict: skip. Solo + regulatory wall = dead.**

## Opportunity B — Building Permits for Solo Trades

### Market
- ~117k HVAC contractors + ~250k+ licensed plumbing/electrical/HVAC firms total
- Q2-2025: 250k+ trade permits per quarter (Shovels Index)
- NAHB 2025: **25.3% of project delays caused by permits** — more than materials, weather, or labor
- Expediter costs $500–2,500 residential, $1,500–5,000 commercial
- Permit-tracking tool adoption: 11% (2022) → 34% (2025) — fast curve

### Competition
- **PermitFlow**: $91M total, $54M Series B March 2026 (Accel), 10x revenue growth, powers $20B construction value — but targets developers/national builders
- **Shovels.ai**: $6.5M seed, 180M permits across 30M addresses, 20,000+ jurisdictions — *data infrastructure*, potential supplier not competitor
- **Symbium**: limited to specific cities (Pinole, Burlingame, Manteca) — narrow
- **GovOS/OpenCounter**: government-side sales (to municipalities)
- **The solo-trade segment is unaddressed** — PermitFlow chases multi-site developers, not the one-truck plumber pulling $200 permits

### Wedge
"Stripe-easy permit pulling for one-truck plumbing/electrical/HVAC shops."

- Focus on top 200 AHJs (~80% of trade-permit volume per Shovels)
- Pricing: $79/mo + $99/permit filed
- Buy permit data from Shovels (cheaper than scraping)
- GTM: r/Plumbing, r/Electricians, r/HVAC, ServiceTitan/Jobber marketplace integrations

### Why permits beats customs cleanly
1. **No licensing wall** (contractor license applies to the contractor, not the vendor)
2. **Incumbents are mis-aimed** (PermitFlow chases $1M+ ACV deals)
3. **Better unit economics for solo**: 250k firms × 1% × $1k ACV = $2.5M ARR achievable solo

### Kill criteria for B
1. **PermitFlow pivots down-market** (~35% probability within 12 months) — they have $54M to deploy
2. **Per-jurisdiction tail unworkable solo** (~30%) — long tail of 19,000+ small AHJs may require bespoke handling
3. **Solo-trade contractors are SaaS-skeptical** (~25%) — Jobber/ServiceTitan penetration still under 40%

### Realistic Outcome
- Solo to $5M ARR: plausible
- $20–40M ARR business: possible with focus on top 200 AHJs + ServiceTitan integration
- $100M ARR solo: structurally hard — PermitFlow will move down-market if you prove the wedge

**Verdict: good $10–30M ARR business; not a $1B path.**
