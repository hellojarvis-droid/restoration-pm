Executive summary

Three reasons this offer reflects the market.

1. The market hasn't grown since the seller bought in 2021. The Uxbridge median sold price in Aug 2021 (when 35 Jonathan transacted) was $1.063M. Today (May 2026) it is $972,000 — flat to slightly down over almost five years, and −21% from the May 2022 peak.

2. The list price has been tested by the market four times and rejected each time. Between Sep 2025 and today the home has been listed for a cumulative 198 days across three consecutive listings (Terminated → Suspended → Active) without an executed offer. The market is telling the seller this home does not transact above $1.0M.

3. The comparable sold record supports $830k–$900k for this specification. Of the ten nearest recent detached sales in Uxbridge, four sold below $900k and the lower quartile is roughly $850k. Our $880k offer sits above the bottom four of those ten sales — it is a fair, market-anchored price.

The framing: We are not trying to extract a discount. We are offering a number the comparable sales record genuinely supports. A sale at $880k preserves a substantial portion of the seller's 2021 cost basis while reflecting four years of flat-to-declining market conditions.
