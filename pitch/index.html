Executive summary

Three reasons this offer reflects the market.

1. The price assumes the wrong size class. 35 Jonathan is a 1,100–1,500 sq ft bungalow. The comparable Uxbridge detached homes that have actually sold in this size + style cohort transacted between $830k and $900k (median ~$865k). The neighbouring $1.2M+ sales the listing implicitly anchors to are all 2,000–3,500 sq ft two-storey homes — literally 2× to 3× the size.

2. The list price has been tested by the market four times and rejected each time. Between Sep 2025 and today the home has been listed for a cumulative 198 days across three consecutive listings (Terminated → Suspended → Active) without an executed offer. The market is telling the seller this home does not transact above $1.0M.

3. The market hasn't grown since the seller bought in 2021. The Uxbridge median sold price in Aug 2021 (when 35 Jonathan transacted) was $1.063M. Today (May 2026) it is $972,000 — −9% from Aug 2021 and −21% from the May 2022 peak. A list price of $1,149,900 implies the home appreciated 17% on a market that depreciated 9%.

The framing: We are not trying to extract a discount. We are offering a number the same-size, same-style comparable sales record genuinely supports. A sale at $880k is above the matched-cohort median, preserves a substantial portion of the seller's 2021 cost basis, and reflects four years of flat-to-declining market conditions.
